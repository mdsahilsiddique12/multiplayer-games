Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 4, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how Game Nexus Studios (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses and protects information when you play Game Nexus (the “Game”) and use any related services, websites or features (collectively, the “Services”).

By accessing or using the Game, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Game.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

  • Account & Login Data: When you sign in (for example, using Firebase Authentication), we may collect an identifier such as your user ID, display name, and email address (if provided by the provider).
  • Gameplay Data: Room codes, match history, scores, roles, XP, in‑game currency, purchased items, and other in‑game actions or progress.
  • Device & Technical Data: IP address, device type, operating system, browser type, language, crash logs, and other technical information used to keep the Game secure and functioning.
  • Analytics Data: Using tools such as Firebase Analytics, we may collect anonymous or aggregated information about how players use the Game (session length, popular modes, retention, etc.).
  • Payment & Purchase Information: When you make in‑app purchases, subscriptions, or buy in‑game currency, the payment is processed by third‑party platforms (such as Google Play, Apple App Store, or payment gateways). We do not store full payment card details ourselves, but we may receive transaction IDs or purchase confirmations.
  • Communication Data: If the Game includes chat, feedback forms, or support messages, we may collect the messages you send so we can moderate and improve the Game.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for purposes such as:

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Game and its features.
  • To create and manage your player profile, XP, rankings, and in‑game inventory.
  • To provide fair matchmaking, rooms, lobbies, and multiplayer sessions.
  • To process in‑app purchases, subscriptions, and in‑game currency transactions.
  • To show in‑game ads (for example via Google AdMob or Unity Ads) in a way that tries not to disturb gameplay.
  • To analyze Game usage so we can balance gameplay, fix bugs, and add new features.
  • To prevent fraud, cheating, and abuse, and to enforce our Terms of Service.
  • To respond to your questions, feedback, and support requests.

3. Ads, Analytics & Tracking

We may integrate third‑party services such as:

  • Google AdMob / Unity Ads: to display advertisements inside the Game.
  • Firebase Analytics or similar tools: to gather anonymous statistics about Game usage.

These providers may use cookies, mobile identifiers, or similar technologies to operate, measure, and improve their services. Their use of data is governed by their own privacy policies.

4. Age Rating & Children

The Game is designed for players aged 9+. Some content may not be suitable for very young children (for example light competitive gameplay, chat features, or in‑app purchases).

If you are under the minimum age required by your country to give consent on your own, you should only use the Game with a parent or guardian’s permission and supervision.

5. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share limited information with:

  • Service Providers: such as hosting providers, analytics tools, payment processors, and ad networks that help us run the Game.
  • Legal & Safety: if required by law, court order, or to protect our rights, other players, or the public (for example when investigating cheating, abuse, or fraud).
  • Business Changes: if we ever merge, sell, or transfer the Game to another entity, your data may transfer as part of the service, while keeping the same level of protection.

6. Data Storage & Security

We store data using third‑party infrastructure providers such as Firebase / Google Cloud. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

However, no system is perfectly secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we try to keep your data as safe as reasonably possible.

7. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your region, you may have rights such as:

  • Accessing the information we have about you.
  • Requesting correction of inaccurate information.
  • Requesting deletion of stored data, where legally allowed.
  • Opting out of certain analytics or personalized ads (where supported).

You can contact us to request these actions (see Contact section below). Some requests may affect your ability to use the Game.

8. In‑App Purchases, Subscriptions & In‑Game Currency

The Game includes:

  • Cosmetic items (skins, VIP status, avatars, effects).
  • In‑game currencies and rewards.
  • Potential subscriptions or season passes with extra content.

All real‑money purchases are handled by the platform’s store or payment provider. Please review their terms and policies for more information about payments and refunds.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the “Last updated” date at the top.

Significant changes may be announced inside the Game or on our website. Continuing to use the Game after changes means you accept the updated Policy.
